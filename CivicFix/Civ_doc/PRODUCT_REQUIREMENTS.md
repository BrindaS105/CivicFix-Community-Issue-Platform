# Product Requirements Document – CivicFix

## 1. Product Overview
CivicFix is a community issue reporting and tracking platform.

## 2. Problem Statement
Residents may lack a convenient way to report local issues
and monitor their progress.

## 3. Product Goal
Make community issue reporting and status tracking simple.

## 4. Target Audience
- Residents
- Community representatives
- Issue management teams

## 5. User Problems
- Reporting processes may be unclear.
- Residents may not know the status of a complaint.
- Multiple types of local problems require tracking.
- Community issue information may be scattered.

## 6. Functional Requirements
- Create a report.
- Select an issue category.
- Enter a location.
- Provide a description.
- View submitted reports.
- Search reports.
- Filter reports.
- Update issue status.
- Delete reports.
- View dashboard metrics.

## 7. Non-Functional Requirements
- Responsive interface.
- Simple navigation.
- Fast interactions.
- Readable information.
- Local data persistence.

## 8. MVP Scope
The MVP focuses on reporting, tracking, filtering and
viewing community issue statistics.

## 9. Out of Scope
- Actual government complaint submission.
- Real-time GPS tracking.
- Verified municipal resolution.
- User authentication.
- Backend database.

## 10. Success Criteria
Users should be able to submit a report, locate it in the
dashboard, update its status and view updated statistics.
