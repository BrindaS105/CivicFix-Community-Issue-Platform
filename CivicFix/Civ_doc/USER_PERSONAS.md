# User Personas – CivicFix

## Persona 1: Local Resident

Needs:
- Report community problems.
- Provide issue location.
- Track report status.

Pain Points:
- Unclear reporting processes.
- Limited visibility into issue progress.

## Persona 2: Community Representative

Needs:
- Understand frequently reported problems.
- Review community concerns.
- Identify unresolved issues.

Pain Points:
- Scattered issue information.
- Difficulty identifying common problems.

## Persona 3: Issue Management Administrator

Needs:
- Review submitted reports.
- Update report statuses.
- Monitor resolution progress.

Pain Points:
- High volume of reports.
- Difficulty prioritizing issues.

## Product Opportunity
CivicFix brings issue reporting and basic status tracking
together in one simple interface.
